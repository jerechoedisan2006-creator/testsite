About Our School

At Greenwood High School, we believe every student deserves a nurturing environment where they can grow academically, socially, and emotionally. Our experienced faculty and modern facilities provide the foundation for lifelong success.

We offer a well-rounded curriculum that balances academics, arts, athletics, and technology, preparing students for the challenges of the future.
