About Us

Learn more about our story, mission, and the people who make Greenwood High School special.

Our Story

Founded in 1995, Greenwood High School began with a simple vision: to create a school where every student is known, valued, and challenged to reach their full potential. What started with just 47 students in a single building has grown into a thriving community of over 1,200 learners.

Over nearly three decades, we've remained committed to academic excellence, character development, and innovation in education. Our graduates go on to top universities, rewarding careers, and meaningful lives of service.

Mission & Values

Our Mission

To empower every student to become a confident, compassionate, and capable leader through rigorous academics, meaningful relationships, and real-world learning experiences.

Core Values

  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in all we do.
  • Integrity: We act with honesty and honor.
  • Respect: We honor the dignity of every individual.
  • Community: We build strong, supportive relationships.
  • Innovation: We embrace creativity and new ideas.

Our Facilities

Modern Classrooms

Every classroom is equipped with smart boards, high-speed internet, and flexible furniture to support collaborative learning.

STEM Laboratory

Our state-of-the-art science and technology lab supports robotics, coding, chemistry, and physics experiments.

Library & Media Center

A quiet, welcoming space with thousands of books, digital resources, and research support for all grade levels.

Athletics Complex

Gymnasium, soccer field, basketball courts, and track support our competitive and recreational sports programs.
